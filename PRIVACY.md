# mailsort privacy policy
Last updated: 19 October 2026

mailsort runs entirely in your browser. It has no server and no account system, and its developers receive none of your data.

**What it reads.** On Gmail and Yahoo Mail pages it reads the sender, subject and preview text of the emails shown in your inbox
list. For Gmail it can also read the unread-mail feed of the account you are signed in to, to alert you to new mail.
This is used only to sort and label mail and to decide whether to notify you.

**What it stores.** Your settings, rules and sender lists, plus what the on-device learner has learned (sender addresses and
hashed word fragments, never the text of your emails). It is kept in your browser's local extension storage on your device,
is not synced, and is deleted when you uninstall the extension or press "Delete everything" in the settings.

**What leaves your device.** Nothing, unless you turn on phone push. If you enter a push topic and approve the permission,
a notification is sent to the ntfy server you specified. By default it contains only the sender's name and category; you can
choose to include the subject, or to send no details at all. Clear the topic to turn it off.

**Sharing.** We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

**Contact:** open a private security advisory or an issue on the project page: https://github.com/nayan-mis/mailsort
