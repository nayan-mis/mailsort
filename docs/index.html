mailsort

Sort your mail.

Download mailsort
Downloaded. Now the forty-second part.
  1. Unzip it. Right-click, Extract. Yes, really.
  2. Paste chrome://extensions into the address bar
    Edge: edge://extensions. Websites aren't allowed to open that page for you.
  3. Switch on Developer mode, hit Load unpacked, pick the mailsort-extension folder.
Then open Gmail. Chrome may grumble about developer mode when it starts. That's Chrome being Chrome. Say keep.
You don't need a damn agent to manage your Gmail inbox. You can do it with an extension too.
