# Code of Conduct

We want mailsort to be a project where people of every background and experience level can contribute without being made to feel unwelcome.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Disagree with the idea, not the person. Criticism of code, design and decisions is welcome when it is specific and constructive.
- Respect other people's time, effort and privacy. Never share someone's personal information, and never paste real email content into an issue.
- Welcome newcomers. Explain rather than dismiss.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or attention, publishing private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and in any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these standards, and may restrict or remove people who do not stop after being asked. To report a problem, use GitHub's private **Report a vulnerability** form on the Security tab, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
